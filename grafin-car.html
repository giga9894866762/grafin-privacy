Grafin Car Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Grafin Car ("the app") is provided by Abdul Rahuman ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details. You sign in with your Graphin ID. The sign-in service gives the app your name, email address and an account identifier so the app can show who you are signed in as.
  • Sign-in tokens. Security tokens that keep you signed in are stored on your device only.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, and it does not show ads or use advertising identifiers.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, keep you signed in, and show your name and email inside the app. We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers.

Where it is processed

Sign-in is handled by the Graphin identity service at id.graphin.online. Data is sent to it over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Keeping and deleting your data

Tokens are removed from your device when you sign out or uninstall the app. To delete your Graphin ID account and the data linked to it, email giga9894866762@gmail.com from the address on the account. We will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The app is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes
