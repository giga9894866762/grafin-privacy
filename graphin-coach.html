Graphin Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Graphin Coach ("the app") is provided by Abdul Rahuman ("we", "us"). It lets students join their coaching institute with an institute code, enrol in a batch, and see attendance and fees. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details. You can sign in with your Graphin ID. The sign-in service gives the app your name, email address and an account identifier so the app can show who you are signed in as.
  • Sign-in tokens. Security tokens that keep you signed in are stored on your device only.
  • Institute data. The app stores the institute you joined, your batch, and the attendance and fee records shown to you. This data is stored on your device only. In this version it is not uploaded to our servers.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, and it does not show ads or use advertising identifiers.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, keep you signed in, and run the features of the app. We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers.

Where it is processed

Sign-in is handled by the Graphin identity service at id.graphin.online. Data is sent to it over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Keeping and deleting your data

Tokens and institute data are removed from your device when you sign out, use "Change institute", or uninstall the app. To delete your Graphin ID account and the data linked to it, email giga9894866762@gmail.com from the address on the account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The app is for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
