Delete your Grafin account

This page applies to all six apps: Grafin UsedCar, Grafin UsedCar Dealer, Grafin Car, Grafin Car Dealer, Grafin CarFind and Grafin CarFind Dealer. They all use one Graphin ID account.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to giga9894866762@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Graphin ID account".
  2. Send it from the email address registered on your account, so we can confirm it is you.
  3. You do not need to give a reason.

What is deleted

Your Graphin ID account and the data linked to it: your name, email address and account identifier. Deleting the account signs you out of all six apps.

What is not kept

Sign-in tokens are stored only on your device. They are removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

How long it takes

We delete the account within 30 days of confirming your request. Deletion cannot be undone.
